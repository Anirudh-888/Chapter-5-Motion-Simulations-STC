Graphical Derivation of Equations of Motion

In NCERT Class 9 Chapter 5 (Motion), the three equations of motion are derived directly from the Velocity-Time (v-t) graph for a body moving with uniform acceleration $a$:

1. First Equation: Velocity-Time Relation ($v = u + at$)
Slope of v-t graph represents Acceleration ($a$):
$$\text{Slope } a = \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t} = \frac{v - u}{t - 0}$$
$$\implies v - u = at \implies \mathbf{v = u + at}$$
2. Second Equation: Position-Time Relation ($s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$)
Area under v-t graph represents Distance/Displacement ($s$):
$$\text{Distance } s = \text{Area of OABC} = \text{Area of Rectangle OADC} + \text{Area of Triangle ABD}$$
$$\text{Area of Rectangle} = OA \times OC = u \times t = ut$$
$$\text{Area of Triangle} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Base} \times \text{Height} = \frac{1}{2} \times t \times (v - u)$$
Since $(v - u) = at$:
$$s = ut + \frac{1}{2} \times t \times (at) \implies \mathbf{s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2}$$
3. Third Equation: Position-Velocity Relation ($2as = v^2 - u^2$)
Using formula for Area of Trapezium OABC:
$$s = \frac{\text{Sum of parallel sides} \times \text{Height}}{2} = \frac{(OA + BC) \times OC}{2} = \frac{(u + v)t}{2}$$
From 1st equation, $t = \frac{v - u}{a}$. Substituting $t$:
$$s = \frac{(v + u)(v - u)}{2a} = \frac{v^2 - u^2}{2a} \implies \mathbf{2as = v^2 - u^2}$$
