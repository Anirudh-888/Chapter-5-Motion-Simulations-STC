What is Speed?

Speed is defined as the rate of change of distance with time. It tells us how fast an object is moving. SI unit is $\text{m/s}$, commercial unit is $\text{km/h}$.

Instantaneous vs Average Speed

Instantaneous Speed is the speed of an object at a specific instant (what the speedometer shows). Average Speed is total distance divided by total time: $v_{\text{avg}} = \frac{s_{\text{total}}}{t_{\text{total}}}$.

Uniform vs Non-Uniform Speed

Uniform Speed: Object covers equal distances in equal intervals of time (straight line on $s\text{-}t$ graph). Non-Uniform Speed: Object covers unequal distances in equal intervals of time.

Class 9 Physics: Speed & Motion Guide

1. Definition of Speed

Speed of a body is the distance travelled by it per unit time:

$$\text{Speed } (v) = \frac{\text{Distance } (s)}{\text{Time } (t)}$$

2. Speedometer vs. Odometer

  • Speedometer: Measures and indicates the instantaneous speed of the vehicle in $\text{km/h}$ or $\text{m/s}$.
  • Odometer: Measures and records the total distance covered by the vehicle.

3. Unit Conversion Formula

To convert from $\text{km/h}$ to $\text{m/s}$, multiply by $\frac{5}{18}$:

$1\text{ km/h} = \frac{1000\text{ m}}{3600\text{ s}} = \frac{5}{18}\text{ m/s} \approx 0.278\text{ m/s}$   |   $1\text{ m/s} = 3.6\text{ km/h}$
